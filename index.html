THE MARKET ISN’T STATIC.
WHY SHOULD YOUR STRATEGY BE?

Traditional backtests test fixed rules against old prices. Backtest Mechanic is designed to investigate changing market conditions and test controlled strategy adaptations—so your trading system can evolve without abandoning your risk boundaries.
