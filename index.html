A PERSONAL WORKSHOP — NOT A SHARED CLOUD DASHBOARD

YOUR MACHINE. YOUR STRATEGIES. YOUR PRIVATE RESEARCH.

Every customer runs a separate installation of Backtest Mechanic on their own computer. Your strategy JSONs, experiments, trade reports and research history stay in your local environment by default. You decide what to import, export or share. The existing local research tools require no mandatory external subscription or shared hosted research session.

Dedicated resources, dedicated attention. The research engine is designed to work in your dedicated installation using the resources available on your machine—not compete in a shared application research queue. Performance depends on your hardware and any optional external services you choose to connect.

Local operation provides control over where research files are stored; it is not a guarantee of anonymity or security. Broker connections, updates and optional integrations can still exchange data externally.

From the workbench to the test track

A complete strategy service history.

Start with a strategy, investigate the losing trades, test repairs against the original, then approve a validated configuration for a compatible bot. The longer-term goal: keep the intelligence engine active after deployment to monitor conditions and reassess the trade thesis within hard risk limits.

01

The Workshop

Define your strategy, indicators, entries, exits and risk rules.

02

Inspection Bay

Run the original backtest and collect trade-level evidence.

03

Diagnostics

Find where and how the strategy loses money.

04

Repair Shop

Propose focused changes and test them individually.

05

The Test Track

Walk-forward, stress test and paper-forward-test.

06

Final Inspection

Document findings; reject, revise or consider deployment.

THE MECHANIC'S RULE: A profitable optimization is not a validated strategy. Previously inspected data cannot be reused as fresh independent proof, and the research process must be willing to reject a weak strategy.
