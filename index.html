More than an if–then backtester

Don't just find out a strategy failed.
Find out why — and what to test next.

Ordinary backtests evaluate predefined rules. Backtest Mechanic is designed as a research workflow: HAL studies the losing trades, proposes focused hypotheses, runs controlled comparisons, keeps a repair history, and checks whether promising changes survive unseen data. It can also tell you when a strategy isn't worth repairing.

⌕

Under-the-Hood Diagnostics

Inspect entries, exits, stop-outs, time-of-day patterns, volatility regimes and trading costs, not just a headline profit figure.

⚒

HAL Repair Consultation

HAL proposes specific fixes. Approve, reject, edit or suggest your own hypothesis before running a separate experiment.

⟳

Controlled Retesting

Compare each modification against the untouched original. Record every version and avoid blind parameter tweaking.

✓

Independent Validation

Walk-forward testing, robustness checks and paper-forward testing help separate durable evidence from curve-fitting.

THE MECHANIC'S RULE: A profitable optimization is not a validated strategy. Previously inspected data cannot be reused as fresh independent proof, and HAL must be willing to recommend abandoning a weak strategy.
