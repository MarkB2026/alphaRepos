About Advanced Chart Lab

A private desktop charting workspace with broker pricing, drawings, technical analysis and chart-based trade planning.

Features & workflow

  • Live broker-connected charts and private drawings
  • Click-to-chart long/short plans with entry, target and stop levels
  • Fibonacci and technical drawing tools
  • Dynamic chart re-sizing...never lose sight of your chart. (see video demos)
  • Candlestick and chart-pattern detection, including triangle development
  • Snapshot-to-backtest workflow and chart feature demonstrations
