Local-first by design

Your strategy belongs on your machine.

Cloud platforms offer convenient hosting. Our approach puts your bot on your own PC, with direct control over when it runs and how it connects.

Typical hosted approach

Cloud-hosted bots

You→Cloud service→Broker

Remote hosting can provide 24/7 uptime without leaving your PC on, but execution depends on the provider and its service availability.

Local bots require your PC, broker connection and internet access to remain available while trading. Broker connections are not configured by default.
