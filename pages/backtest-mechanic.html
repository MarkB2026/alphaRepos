About Backtest Mechanic

Build, test and investigate strategies against historical data. Results are research evidence, not proof of future profitability.

Features & workflow

  • Historical backtesting with trade-level results and charts
  • Snapshot and strategy JSON workflows
  • Ollama natural-language demonstration
  • Walk-forward and holdout evaluation are important validation practices
