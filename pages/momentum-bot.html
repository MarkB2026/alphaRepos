Features & workflow

  • Manual: The trader selects and controls trading decisions.
  • Hybrid: The bot identifies opportunities while the trader approves or supervises execution.
  • Fully Autonomous: The bot can independently identify opportunities, decide when to enter, manage positions and exits, according to its implemented strategy.
  • Momentum scanner and ARM confirmation workflow
  • Automatic, full-cycle, long-only and short-only operating modes
  • Configurable strategy presets and opening-candle price-action triggers
  • Trade management including protective exits and end-of-day flattening options
  • Live broker behavior must be validated for the distributed build
