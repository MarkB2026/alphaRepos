About Option Price Plotter

Move the Price. Move the Date. See Your P&L. Instantly.

Not Just Another Options Payoff Chart.

Sure, most options plotters let you select a date before expiration and examine a theoretical price curve. But then you're left searching along that curve to find your projected stock price and figure out what your position might be worth.

Option Price Plotter takes that a step further.

Use the sliders to adjust both the projected stock price and future date, or change your projected stock price directly on the chart. Your theoretical option value and P&L recalculate instantly with every adjustment.

No repeatedly entering dates. No hunting along payoff curves. No switching between calculations.

Just two questions:

  • Where do you expect the stock price to be?
  • How many days do you think it will take to get there?

Option Price Plotter shows you what your position could be worth under those conditions, accounting for time remaining and implied volatility.

Explore technical price targets, expected moves, and potential pricing imbalances—all on the fly.

Features & workflow

  • Interactive Price Plotting — Move the projected future price directly on the chart to explore different outcomes.
  • Future Date Projection — Select a future date and estimate what your options could be worth at that time.
  • Live Scenario Updates — See projected values change as you adjust your price scenario.
  • Before-Expiration Analysis — Explore potential outcomes before your options reach expiration.
  • Profit & Loss Visualization — Understand potential gains, losses, and breakeven points.
  • Risk/Reward Planning — Explore price targets and holding periods before placing a trade.

Your chart. Your price target. Your timeline. Don't just calculate an options scenario. Plot it.
