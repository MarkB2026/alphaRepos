HAL Think Box — Give Your Bot Custom Thinking

Two ways to customize your thinking: use the adaptive Custom Pattern Rule editor to define supported pattern-entry conditions, or experiment with the Plain English (Beta) tab by describing a trade idea in everyday language.

Custom Pattern Rule: select a supported pattern and configure its measurements, including touches, swing confirmation, breakout closes, relative volume and trading-session limits. Where supported by the bot, saved and enabled pattern rules are evaluated before entries and may block trades that fail their conditions.

Plain English (Beta): a limited local phrase interpreter recognizes selected pattern conditions and creates an editable JSON draft. Press Preview JSON to generate it, then edit the JSON yourself. This tab does not use an AI language model; it does not save, validate or submit the draft to the trading engine.

Explore the two screenshots in the HAL Think Box feature card. More patterns, language capabilities and customization are planned as they are built and tested. Feature availability varies by bot and edition. Pattern recognition and automation cannot guarantee trading results.
