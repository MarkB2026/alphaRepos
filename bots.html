Specialized Bots

Focused strategies and dedicated trading tools

Each bot has its own purpose, feature set and release status. Capabilities marked as planned are not presented as available until integration testing is complete.

Built around the whole trade

Finding the trade is only the beginning. Managing it matters.

Each bot is tailored to a specific trading style, while giving you extensive settings to customize how it behaves. Entry signals can be fickle. Once a position is open, the focus shifts to staying with trades that are working, protecting profits as they develop, and getting out when they are not — with the goal of preserving as much profit and limiting as much loss as the configured strategy allows.
