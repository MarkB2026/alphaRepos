EXAMPLE 01 · ONE CONVERSATION

From a trading idea to a complete plan

YOU TELL HAL
“I think gold is going to run after the jobs report. Set up a bullish trade plan and scan for the best symbols.”

HAL would interpret this as a bullish gold thesis—not an instruction to buy immediately. First, it would verify the economic catalyst and look for evidence that supports or contradicts the idea.

EXAMPLE 02 · WEBSITE FEATURE VISION

One conversation. A complete trading plan.

Imagine telling HAL: “I think gold is going to run after the jobs report. Set up a trade plan and scan for the best symbols.”

HAL could turn that one sentence into an actionable trading workflow.

01 · Understand your thesis

Interpret your outlook, identify the catalyst and distinguish a prediction from a confirmed opportunity.

02 · Research the market

Check economic releases, news, price action, volume, Treasury yields and dollar strength.

03 · Scan for opportunities

Rank eligible symbols using live broker data, liquidity, momentum and your preferred strategies.

04 · Build your trade plan

Propose entries, protective stops, profit targets, invalidation levels and risk/reward.

05 · Explain every decision

Show why each candidate qualifies and what evidence could invalidate the thesis.

06 · You remain in control

Review, modify, approve or reject. Future integrations could pass approved plans to compatible bots for paper testing or execution.

Your instinct. HAL's analysis. Your decision.

WHAT EXISTS TODAY

HAL Think Box is the foundation

Today's early-beta Think Box already demonstrates two pieces of this longer-term direction: a configurable pattern-rule editor and a limited Plain English-to-JSON preview. Neither should be confused with the future conversational research and trade-planning system shown above.

HAL Think Box — Plain English (Beta) ↗ HAL Think Box — Custom Pattern Rule Editor ↗

Current Plain English Beta uses a limited phrase interpreter, not a language model. Its editable JSON is preview-only and is not saved or submitted to the engine. Supported saved pattern rules are a separate capability.
