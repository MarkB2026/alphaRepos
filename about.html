WHY Trading Bot?

Your portfolio shouldn't require your full-time attention.

Markets move all day. Your trading strategies shouldn't depend on whether you're sitting in front of six monitors when a setup appears. Trading Bots brings compatible trading bots together so they can monitor predefined strategies while you keep visibility and control.

A DIFFERENT WAY TO MANAGE

Why hand every decision to someone else when automation can execute the rules you choose?

Traditional portfolio management can involve management fees, periodic decision-making and less direct control over individual trades. Trading Bots takes a different approach: you choose the bots and strategies, they run through your configured brokerage connection, and the Control Center gives you one place to monitor what they're doing.

WHY AUTOMATE?

Let software do what software is good at.

Automation doesn't predict the future and it doesn't remove market risk. What it can do is repeatedly monitor data, evaluate defined conditions and execute configured rules without getting tired, distracted or needing to be at a desk.

◷

Your time

A bot can keep monitoring its strategy while you're working, sleeping or doing something more useful than staring at charts.

⌁

Data & rules

Strategies can evaluate incoming market information against predefined conditions instead of relying on a rushed manual decision when an opportunity appears.

◎

Consistency

Once configured, software can apply the same execution logic repeatedly without hesitation, distraction or fat-finger order-entry mistakes.

HUMAN MANAGEMENT VS. BOT MANAGEMENT

You're not replacing judgment. You're choosing where to use it.

A human still chooses the strategy, risk settings and whether automation should be running. The difference is that the repetitive monitoring and execution can be delegated to software.

Traditional / manual approach

  • A person reviews information and decides when to act
  • Monitoring is limited by time and attention
  • Manual execution can require constant screen time
  • Managed products may charge management or advisory fees

Trading Bot approach

  • You select and configure rule-based strategies
  • Bots can continuously monitor supported market conditions while running
  • Configured actions can be executed automatically where integration is supported and tested
  • You retain visibility through your bots, broker and central Control Center

FROM ONE BOT TO A Trading Bot

Different strategies. One control center.

A standalone bot can automate one strategy. A Trading Bot adds a management layer around compatible bots so you can organize them together, monitor activity and coordinate ownership rather than treating every bot like an isolated program.

LOCAL FIRST

Your computer. Your brokerage. Your configuration.

Trading Bots is built around downloadable applications designed to run locally. You configure the strategies and risk controls. Broker APIs and other connected features require their own setup, and feature availability varies by bot and release.

A practical note: Automation changes how trades are monitored and executed; it does not eliminate financial risk. Trading bots can encounter software failures, connectivity interruptions, slippage, rejected orders and unexpected market conditions. Begin in WATCH or PAPER mode, understand each strategy and verify broker integration before considering LIVE trading. Trading Bots does not guarantee investment results.
