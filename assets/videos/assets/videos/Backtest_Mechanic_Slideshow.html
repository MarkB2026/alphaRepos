What the demo covers

  1. Define the strategy and run the original historical backtest.
  2. Review trade-level diagnostics and changing market conditions.
  3. Test a controlled parameter repair against the original.
  4. Examine validation limitations before accepting any research proposal.
  5. Export a research-only JSON proposal for explicit review in a compatible bot.

Full autonomous repair and historical HAL replay are planned capabilities, not demonstrated production features.
